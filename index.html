About the Firm

About Us

M/s Shivam Constructions is a proprietorship construction firm established in 2014, owned and managed by Mr. Bal Kishan Sharma. The firm specializes in road construction and civil infrastructure works, with a strong focus on quality execution and timely project delivery.

Under the leadership of Mr. Bal Kishan Sharma, the firm has successfully executed multiple government projects across Mathura, Vrindavan, Hathras (Uttar Pradesh), and Ashok Nagar (Madhya Pradesh). The firm has built a reputation for reliable workmanship and compliance with approved technical specifications.

We have worked with several government bodies, including the Public Works Department (PWD), Rural Engineering Department (RED), Jal Kal Vibhag, Nagar Nigam Mathura–Vrindavan, and the Mathura Vrindavan Vikas Pradhikaran. Our work focuses on delivering durable, safe, and high-quality infrastructure while strictly following approved specifications, safety norms, and timelines.
